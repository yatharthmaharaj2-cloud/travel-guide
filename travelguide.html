🌏 Welcome to India

A land of incredible diversity

India is a vast and culturally diverse country where ancient traditions meet modern cities. Travelers can explore Himalayan landscapes, deserts, beaches, forests, historic monuments, spiritual centers and vibrant markets.


Every region has its own food, languages, architecture, festivals and traditions. Whether you are interested in history, nature, adventure, spirituality or cuisine, India offers an experience for every kind of traveler.

📍 Must-Visit Destinations

Choose your adventure

🏛️

Agra

Home of the magnificent Taj Mahal, Agra is one of India's most famous heritage destinations. Explore the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and the city's Mughal history.

HERITAGE
🏰

Jaipur

Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is famous for grand palaces, forts, colorful bazaars and Rajasthan's rich royal heritage.

ROYAL INDIA
🌊

Goa

Goa combines beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, seafood, nightlife and a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

BEACHES
🌴

Kerala

Kerala is known for lush landscapes, backwaters, beaches, traditional cuisine and wellness experiences.

NATURE
🏔️

Ladakh

A high-altitude Himalayan region famous for dramatic mountains, monasteries, clear skies and adventure.

ADVENTURE
🍵

Darjeeling

Famous for Himalayan views, tea gardens and the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, Darjeeling offers a beautiful mountain escape.

MOUNTAINS

🍛 Taste of India

Every region has its own flavor

🍚

Biryani

Aromatic rice cooked with spices and vegetables or meat. Different regions have their own styles.

🥞

Dosa

A crisp South Indian crepe traditionally served with sambar and chutneys.

🥘

Rajasthani Thali

A colorful meal featuring a variety of curries, breads, rice and sweets.

🥟

Momos

Popular dumplings enjoyed particularly across Himalayan and northeastern regions.

🍮

Gulab Jamun

Soft fried milk-based sweets soaked in a fragrant sugar syrup.

🫖

Masala Chai

Spiced tea prepared with milk and aromatic ingredients such as cardamom and ginger.

🎒 Smart Travel Tips

Things to know before you go

📅

Plan around the weather

India has many different climates. Research the season and local conditions for each destination before planning your trip.

💳

Carry payment options

Keep a combination of cards and some local currency. Payment methods can vary between cities and smaller destinations.

🙏

Respect local customs

Dress appropriately when visiting religious places, follow local rules and ask before photographing people.

📱

Stay connected

Keep your phone charged and save important addresses, tickets and emergency information offline.

🛂

Check visa requirements

International visitors should check the latest visa and immigration requirements before traveling.

🚨

Know emergency numbers

India's national emergency number is 112. The official tourist helpline is 1363.
